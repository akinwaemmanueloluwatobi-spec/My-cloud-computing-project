THEOLOGICAL FOUNDATIONS

What We Believe

Rooted in historical orthodox Christianity, centered on the Lord Jesus Christ, and united in essential truths.

Note: Below are our core faith categories. Detailed theological statements will be provided in the official MASS Movement UK Statement of Faith document upon final governance review.

We affirm Jesus Christ as the eternal Son of God, Lord of all creation, crucified for our sins, bodily resurrected, and exalted above every name. Everything MASS Movement UK does flows from devotion to Him.

[CLIENT TO PROVIDE OFFICIAL STATEMENT OF FAITH — JESUS CHRIST]

We hold to the supreme authority, inspiration, and trustworthiness of the Holy Scriptures as the inspired Word of God for faith, life, and ministry.

[CLIENT TO PROVIDE OFFICIAL STATEMENT OF FAITH — SCRIPTURE]

We believe in the active presence, power, and gifts of the Holy Spirit who empowers believers for holy living, passionate worship, and bold witness.

[CLIENT TO PROVIDE OFFICIAL STATEMENT OF FAITH — THE HOLY SPIRIT]

We affirm the one universal Body of Christ composed of all true believers across denominations, local churches, traditions, and nations.

[CLIENT TO PROVIDE OFFICIAL STATEMENT OF FAITH — THE CHURCH]

Salvation is by grace alone, through faith alone, in Jesus Christ alone — bringing redemption, reconciliation with God, and eternal life.

[CLIENT TO PROVIDE OFFICIAL STATEMENT OF FAITH — SALVATION]

We are sent by Christ into all the world to preach the Gospel, make disciples, care for the vulnerable, and demonstrate the Kingdom of God.

[CLIENT TO PROVIDE OFFICIAL STATEMENT OF FAITH — MISSION]

We actively foster unity in diversity — bringing together Pentecostal, Evangelical, Anglican, Baptist, and historical streams in genuine brotherly love.

[CLIENT TO PROVIDE OFFICIAL STATEMENT OF FAITH — UNITY]

Prayer is the engine of the movement. We cultivate personal and corporate prayer hubs across the UK for spiritual awakening.

[CLIENT TO PROVIDE OFFICIAL STATEMENT OF FAITH — PRAYER]
