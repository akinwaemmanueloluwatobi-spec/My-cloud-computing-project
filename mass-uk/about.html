About MASS Movement UK

Jesus. To Every Generation. To Every Nation.

Our Heart & Vision

Gathered in Unity. Sent in Mission.

MASS Movement UK is a national interdenominational Christian movement with one singular, uncompromising vision: to carry the name of Jesus to every generation and every nation.

We exist because we believe the Church in the United Kingdom is strongest when it stands united around the Person and gospel of Jesus Christ — transcending denominational barriers, cultural divisions, and generational divides.

A Movement, Not Another Church

We are not a local church or a new denomination. MASS Movement UK serves as a catalyst and national network that gathers believers from Pentecostal, Anglican, Baptist, Independent, and historic fellowships across the country. We do not replace local churches; we champion them, strengthen them, and rally them around the supreme name of Jesus.

Championing the Next Generation

We hold a conviction that young people are not merely the Church of tomorrow — they are vital leaders in the Church of today. MASS UK intentionally creates spaces, gatherings, and mentoring pathways that empower young Christians to step into leadership, boldness, and Kingdom mission.

Connected to a Global Family

While independently governed in the United Kingdom, MASS UK is anchored within the global MASS Movement family. Together, we share the same heartbeat, core values, and God-given mandate: to see Jesus made known in every nation.
